# Employee Handbook (Sample)

## Leave Policy

Employees receive 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per year, accrued monthly
at a rate of 1.67 days per month. Unused PTO of up to 5 days may roll over
into the next calendar year; anything beyond that is forfeited.

Sick leave is separate from PTO: employees receive 10 paid sick days per
year, which do not roll over.

## Expense Policy

Travel and business expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the
expense date, with itemized receipts attached for any single expense over
$25. Expenses submitted after 30 days require manager approval to be
reimbursed.

## Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week without prior approval.
Fully remote arrangements require sign-off from both the employee's manager
and HR, and are reviewed every 6 months.

## Refunds and Reimbursements

Approved expense reimbursements are processed within 14 business days of
approval, paid via the same method as regular payroll.
